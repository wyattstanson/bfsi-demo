How to use Aria

One decision engine, five points of view. Switch views from the top bar at any time.

Concierge, the customer. Pick a profile and press Show my moment. Ask Ava anything, from budgeting and credit to investing and fraud, or ask it to handle a loan, a dispute, or a payment.
Co-pilot, the advisor. A ready, spoken brief: the recommended action, the drivers, and talking points.
Control Tower, the executive. The book of decisions: value, latency, fairness, and a live stream by action and region.
Assurance, the regulator. Every decision explained with a SHAP force plot, a fairness ratio, and the audit record.
Engine Room, the engineer. The technical view, behind a passcode.
Tip: the speaker icon reads aloud exactly what is on screen. The moon icon switches light and dark. Ava always answers, even offline.
